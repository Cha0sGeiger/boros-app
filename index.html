The forecast

Everything you check, nothing you don't.

One screen, top to bottom: what it's like now, the next 24 hours, the next two hours of rain, the details and seven days ahead.

  • Next 2 hours in 15-minute steps, with a plain sentence: "Dry for the next two hours."
  • A headline that helps: bring a jacket, grab an umbrella, gusts later.
  • Feels like, wind, humidity, pressure, UV, rain today and sunrise and sunset.
  • Seven days with highs, lows and a red chance of rain when it matters.
  • Your places: swipe between your location and the cities you save.
  • Share a day as a picture with whoever you're meeting.

Best time for…

The best window, found for you.

Boros reads the next two days and picks the best time for a run, a ride, a walk with the baby, drying laundry, a barbecue or photos. Tell it the temperatures you like and it plans around them.

Up next & tracking

What's coming, on the main page.

Calendar events with a place get a leave-by time. Any trip you plan can be tracked there too, one at a time, until it's done.

Trips

Weather along the whole way.

Plan a drive, a stay somewhere or a flight. Boros checks departures across your window, picks the best one and shows the weather at each point of the route, in local time.

  • Routes, with up to two stops on the way: the best time to leave, with ice, rain and wind along the road.
  • Places: what it will be like while you're there.
  • Flights: delay risks at each airport and what to wear when you land.
  • Track it: keep one trip on the main page. Boros checks it once a day, and more often as it gets close.

From your calendar

Open an event, see when to go.

Events with a place open on what matters: when to leave, how long it takes, and what the weather does on the way. Change the route or the time underneath if you need to.

  • Leave-by time with ten minutes to spare, or a heads-up when you're running late.
  • Flights: write Flight to Split as the title and the airport you leave from as the location, with takeoff and landing as the times.
  • Only on your phone: Boros reads your calendar on the device and nothing leaves it.

Rhythm & Daybreak

Your week, with a verdict for each part.

Add work, the commute, runs and plans as beats in your week. Each one gets a verdict: good, umbrella, windy, cold.

Daybreak brings it all together in one short brief each morning, at the time you choose.

Make it yours

Twenty-six themes. Here's a taste.

Every theme brings its own colours and type, down to the dots in the graph. Keep one, follow light and dark, switch at sunrise and sunset, shuffle daily or let the weather choose.

Classic. Black, white and one red dot. Where Boros started.

Plus temperature styles, six icon sets, your own accent and a stronger-contrast mode in every theme.

Widgets

Thirteen widgets. Each one answers one thing.

Home screen, lock screen and StandBy, in your theme and kept fresh in the background. Nine of them are new.

  • Week on paper: the week ahead as quiet ink columns on paper, every day drawn to the same scale.
  • Month stripes: this month's daily highs as colour stripes, from what Boros has seen where you are. A new stripe every day.
  • Now, Rain, Day and 7 days: the temperature in dot type, the two-hour rain countdown, today in full and the week ahead, on the home and lock screen.

Now and Thermometer are free. The rest come with Boros Pro.

Free & Pro

The forecast is free. Always.

Boros is made by one person. No ads, no tracking, no data sold. Pro unlocks planning and every look, monthly, yearly (with a free week to try it) or once for good.

Free

Everything you need to know the weather.

  • The full forecast: now, 24 hours with time-travel, next 2 hours, details, 7 days
  • Alerts and the rain countdown Live Activity
  • Air quality and pollen
  • Now and Thermometer widgets and Siri questions
  • Three themes: Classic, Paper and Aurora
  • Your location plus three places
  • All accessibility settings

Boros Pro

Plan around the weather, and make it yours.

  • All 26 themes, temperature styles, icon sets, accents, theme modes and sounds
  • Rhythm and Daybreak: your week and a brief every morning
  • Calendar autopilot and trips: routes with stops, places, flights and a tracked trip
  • Best time for… runs, rides, walks, laundry and more
  • Every widget, the Apple Watch app and complications
  • Unlimited places

Privacy

Your weather stays yours.

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Rounded coordinates go to the weather service; your location is never stored on a server.
  • Calendar events are read only on your phone to plan trips, and never leave it.
  • Anonymous usage counts help improve Boros, and you can switch them off in Settings.
